# Lab notebook: starter trial (example data)

This is an invented example for a demo. The numbers are made up.

We want to know whether a warmer proof gives a more sour loaf. Sam thinks it does, because the bacteria that make acid grow faster when warm.

Over two weeks we baked twelve loaves. Six proofed at 22 C and six at 30 C. The 30 C loaves had a mean pH of 3.8 and the 22 C loaves a mean pH of 4.3. Tasters rated the 30 C loaves more sour in five of six pairs.

One thing does not fit. The two 30 C loaves made with the oldest starter were no more sour than their 22 C pairs. So starter age may matter as much as temperature.
